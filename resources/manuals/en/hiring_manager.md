# Manual for hiring managers

You are a **Hiring Manager** at an employer on BLUESKILLS. You look at jobs and applicants, book interviews and record how they went, keep shortlists and talk to applicants. You can't post jobs, move applicants between stages, make offers or reject applicants.

## Jobs

**What it is for:** see your organization's jobs and the people who applied, and book interviews.

**How to open it:** click **Jobs** in the sidebar. Click a job to open its details. Your role can't post, edit, pause or close jobs.

### Applicants

Click **Applicants** on a job to see who applied.

- **Pipeline stages** at the top filter the list by stage.
- Each row shows the worker, **Requirements** met (for example "4 of 5"), the stage, who handles it and when they applied.
- Click an applicant to see the **Skill Passport at application** (a dated copy that later edits don't change), the documents the worker shared and the **History**.

### Book an interview

1. Open the applicant and click **Book interview**.
2. Choose the **Date and time** (in the future), the **Length (minutes)** (10 to 240) and **How**: in person, video call or phone call.
3. For an in-person interview, add the **Address**. For a call, add the **Call link or number**.
4. Add **Instructions for the worker** and save. The worker sees it in their application, in their own time zone.

### Record the outcome

After the interview, choose the **Outcome**: Passed, Not passed, Did not attend or Cancelled. Add a note if you like and save.

Moving applicants, rejecting them and making offers are done by recruiters and HR admins. Tell them the outcome.

## Talent

The sidebar shows **Talent**, but talent search is for recruiters, HR admins and owners. If you open it, BLUESKILLS says you don't have access. Ask a recruiter to find workers for you.

## Shortlists

**What it is for:** keep lists of workers your team is considering. A list gives nobody extra access, and workers are not told.

**How to open it:** click **Shortlists** in the sidebar.

### Create a shortlist

1. Click **New shortlist**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, choose a job in **For job**.
3. Click **Create list**.

### Work with a list

- Open a list to see its workers and the notes your team added.
- Click **Remove** to take someone off the list.
- Click **Delete list** and confirm to delete the list. Workers are not told.

Workers are added to lists from talent search, which recruiters use.

## Common questions

**I can't move an applicant or make an offer.** Your role books interviews and records outcomes. Ask a recruiter or HR admin to move, reject or make offers.

**Talent search says I don't have access.** That is expected for your role.

**The interview time looks wrong.** Times are in your own time zone. The worker sees them in theirs.

**A worker's phone number is hidden.** Workers choose what to share. Contact them through **Messages**.
