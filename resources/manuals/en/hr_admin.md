## Common questions

**I can't submit a job for review.** Your organization must be verified first. Ask an owner to upload the documents BLUESKILLS asked for. Also fix every item under **Fix these before submitting**.

**I don't see Billing.** Billing is for owners only.

**Why did my live job disappear from search?** You changed the pay, the worker costs or the conditions, so it went back to review. It comes back once it is approved.

**I can't make an offer.** Add a salary to the job first. The offer can't be lower than the Truth Card salary, and only one offer can wait for an answer at a time.

**I can't change the last owner's role.** An organization needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first.

**A worker's phone number or passport scan is hidden.** Workers choose what to share and can stop sharing at any time. Contact them through **Messages**.
