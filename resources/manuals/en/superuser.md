# Manual for superusers

You are a **Superuser** on the BLUESKILLS staff. You can use every staff page: all review queues, trust and safety, CRM, marketing and billing, plus configuration, occupations, the audit log, the staff team, and the roles and permissions of everyone in BLUESKILLS. You can also read the user manual of every role: choose a role in **Manual for** at the top of this page.

## Design system and Queues

- **Design system** is a gallery of the screen parts BLUESKILLS is built from, for the product team.
- **Queues** opens the background job monitor (Horizon). Sign-in codes, Voice-to-CV answers, SkillShow videos and notifications are processed there. Check it when workers say codes or videos are slow.

## User Manual

This guide. Click **User Manual** in the sidebar to open it.

- **Manual for** lets you open the manual of any role, grouped by workers, employer roles, agency roles, institute roles and BLUESKILLS staff. Other people only ever see their own role's manual.
- **Contents** lists every part of the manual. Click a heading to jump to it.
- **Search this manual** finds a word in the open manual.
- **Print** prints the whole manual, without the menu and buttons.

## Common questions

**A staff member lost their phone and recovery codes.** Check it is really them, then click **Reset two-factor** next to their name in **Staff**. They set it up again at their next sign-in.

**I can't deactivate a staff member or change their role.** BLUESKILLS needs at least one Superuser, and you can't deactivate yourself.

**I changed corridor rules but an open case didn't change.** Rules apply to jobs and cases from now on. Open cases keep their stages.

**I can't remove an occupation or skill.** It is in use. Rename it instead.

**Someone says they can't do something their manual describes.** Their role may have been changed. Open **Roles & permissions** and look for **Changed** on their role.

**How do I see who changed something?** Open **Audit log** and filter by record ID or person.
