# Manual for compliance reviewers

You are a **Compliance Reviewer** at a recruitment agency on BLUESKILLS. You keep your agency's licence and mandates in order and watch compliance. You can look at job orders, candidates, mobility cases and messages, but you don't post jobs or handle applicants.

## Common questions

**My mandate request was refused straight away.** Your licence is expired or doesn't cover the destination. Add the destination in **Licence**, or contact BLUESKILLS if the licence itself was renewed.

**BLUESKILLS rejected a mandate.** Read the reason on the mandate, for example the letter isn't signed or doesn't match the trades, destination or headcount. Request a new mandate with a corrected letter.

**Job orders show under "Job orders without a usable mandate".** Get a new mandate for that employer. Once it is verified, a case officer can resume the jobs.

**I can't change job orders or applicants.** That is expected: your role can only look at them.
