# Manual for employer owners

You are an **Owner** of an employer on BLUESKILLS. You can do everything in your organization: register and verify it, post jobs, hire workers, follow their visas and travel, manage your team and see billing.

## Common questions

**I can't submit a job for review.** Your organization must be verified first. Check the notice at the top of your workspace. Also fix every item under **Fix these before submitting**.

**Talent search says it opens after verification.** BLUESKILLS must approve your organization first. If it is approved and you see that talent search isn't part of your plan, ask BLUESKILLS to add it.

**Why did my live job disappear from search?** You changed the pay, the worker costs or the conditions, so it went back to review. It comes back once it is approved.

**I can't make an offer.** Add a salary to the job first. The offer can't be lower than the salary on the Truth Card, and only one offer can wait for an answer at a time.

**I can't remove myself or change my role.** An organization needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first.

**A worker's phone number or passport scan is hidden.** Workers choose what to share with each application and can stop sharing at any time. Contact them through **Messages**.

**Who are the other roles?** **HR Admin** can do everything except billing and changing the organization's registration. **Recruiter** runs hiring but can't manage the team. **Hiring Manager** looks at applicants, books interviews and keeps shortlists. **Viewer** can only look.
