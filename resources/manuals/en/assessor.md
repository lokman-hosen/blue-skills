# Manual for assessors

You are an **Assessor** at a training institute on BLUESKILLS. You record the results of trade tests and confirm (attest) that graduates completed their training. You can look at courses, cohorts and graduates but not change them.

## Cohorts

**What it is for:** every intake of your courses, with the graduates who joined.

**How to open it:** click **Cohorts** in the sidebar, then click a cohort. You can record assessments and attest training from the list of graduates on the cohort page. Your role can't edit cohorts or invite graduates.

## Common questions

**The Record assessment button is missing.** The graduate must have accepted the invitation, and BLUESKILLS must have verified your institute's accreditation.

**A graduate failed. Will employers see it?** No. Only a pass appears on the Skill Passport. Other results stay between your institute and the graduate.

**I recorded the wrong result.** Click **Withdraw** on it, write the reason, then record the right result.
