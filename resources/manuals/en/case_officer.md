# Manual for case officers

You are a **Case Officer** at a recruitment agency on BLUESKILLS. You post job orders under your agency's mandates, look after candidates, book interviews, make offers and run hired workers' visas and travel. Mandates, the licence, the team and billing are handled by the agency owner and compliance reviewer.

## Common questions

**I can't post a job order.** Your agency needs a verified mandate from the employer and a valid licence for the destination. Ask your agency owner or compliance reviewer.

**The Mandate list in the job form is empty or a mandate is "no longer usable".** The mandate expired, was revoked, or is full. Ask your agency owner or compliance reviewer for a new one.

**A job order was paused by itself.** Its mandate lapsed. When a new mandate is in place, open the job and click **Resume**.

**I can't change a mobility stage.** Only the current stage can be changed. If it is on hold, click **Resume** first.

**I can't close a case as joined.** Finish or skip every stage first.
