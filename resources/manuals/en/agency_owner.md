# Manual for agency owners

You are an **Agency Owner** on BLUESKILLS. Your agency recruits workers for employers under a licence. You can do everything in your agency: register and verify it, keep the licence and mandates in order, post job orders, hire workers, run their visas and travel, manage your team and see billing.

## Common questions

**I can't post a job order.** You need a verified mandate from the employer and a valid licence that covers the destination. Check **Mandates** and **Licence**.

**My mandate request was refused straight away.** Your licence is expired or doesn't cover the destination. Add the destination in **Licence**, or contact BLUESKILLS if the licence itself was renewed.

**A job order was paused by itself.** Its mandate ended or was revoked, or your licence stopped covering the destination. See **Compliance**, get a new mandate, then click **Resume**.

**The openings are too high.** A job can't have more openings than the places left on its mandate. Lower the openings or ask the employer for a bigger mandate.

**Who are the other roles?** A **Case Officer** runs job orders, candidates and mobility cases. A **Compliance Reviewer** keeps the licence and mandates in order and checks compliance, and can only look at job orders and candidates.
