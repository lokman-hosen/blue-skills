## Common questions

**I can't decide without a reason.** Asking for more information or rejecting always needs a reason. Approving doesn't.

**The worker document won't open.** Click **Start review** first. The file opens only after the review is assigned to you.

**I want the worker to upload a better scan.** Reject with a reason such as "unreadable". The worker sees it and can upload again.

**This review was already decided.** Someone else decided it. Go back to the queue and take the next item.

**A job keeps coming back.** Jobs come back whenever the employer changes pay, costs or conditions after publishing. Review them again.
