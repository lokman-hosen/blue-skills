## Common questions

**I can't decide an appeal.** You took the action it is about. Another trust officer must decide it.

**I can't suspend a job.** Only live or paused jobs can be suspended. Reject jobs still in review from **Job reviews**.

**I can't suspend an organization.** Only verified organizations can be suspended. Reject registrations that aren't verified yet from the verification queue (Verification Officers).

**The report says it isn't about anything that still exists.** The job, organization or worker was removed. Dismiss the report with a reason.

**What does the reporter see?** Only whether action was taken. Never your notes or the evidence.
