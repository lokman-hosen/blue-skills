## Settings

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, or click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and then **Settings**.

- **Profile:** change your name or email address and click **Save**.
- **Security:** change your password, see your recovery codes (**Show recovery codes**, **Make new codes**) and add or remove passkeys. Staff can't use BLUESKILLS without two-factor authentication, so keep your authenticator app and recovery codes safe.
- **Appearance:** choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **Notification settings:** open the bell in the top bar and click **Notification settings** to choose which notifications also come by email.

If you lose your phone and your recovery codes, tell a Superuser. After checking it is really you, they reset your two-factor authentication from the Staff page, and you set it up again at your next sign-in.
