## Billing

**What it is for:** plans, posting credits and invoices for organizations. Workers are never charged by BLUESKILLS, and platform billing is kept separate from any cost a worker pays for a job. Payments are recorded by hand.

**How to open it:** click **Billing** in the sidebar. Use the tabs **Organizations**, **Invoices** and **Plans**.

### Plans

1. Open **Plans** and click **New plan** (or **Edit** on a plan).
2. Enter the **Name**, a **Short description**, **For** (employer, agency or institute), **Billed** (per month, per year or one-off), **Price**, **Currency**, **Posting credits**, **Seats** (leave empty for unlimited), and tick **Includes talent search** and **Includes recruitment projects** as needed.
3. Tick **Offered now** to make it available.
4. Save.

Plans are switched off, never deleted, so old invoices keep their plan. Prices and regulated recruitment charges need approval before launch.

### Put an organization on a plan

1. In **Organizations**, search for the organization and open it.
2. Click **Change plan**, choose the plan and the start date.
3. Tick **Create a draft invoice for the first period** if needed.
4. Click **Put on this plan**. The current plan ends the day before. The plan's posting credits are added when its invoice is paid.

### Adjust posting credits

1. Open the organization and click **Adjust credits**.
2. Enter the **Change**: a positive number adds credits, a negative number removes them (not zero, up to 10,000).
3. Write the **Reason** and click **Save adjustment**.

### Create and issue an invoice

1. Open the organization and click **New invoice**. A draft is created.
2. Click **Add a line**. Enter the description, quantity, unit price and, if it sells credits, **Posting credits per unit (optional)**. Add as many lines as needed. Click **Remove** to delete a line.
3. Click **Issue invoice** and confirm. It gets its number and due date, can't be changed afterwards, and the organization's billing managers are told.

### Record a payment

1. Open the invoice and click **Record payment**.
2. Choose **Paid by** (bank transfer, card, cash or other), enter the **Bank or payment reference** and **Paid on** (today or earlier).
3. Save. Posting credits on the invoice are added to the organization.

### Void an invoice

Click **Void invoice**, write **Why it's void** and confirm.

The **Invoices** tab lists every invoice; **Overdue** ones also show on the Overview.
