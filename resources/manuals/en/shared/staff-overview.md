## Overview

**What it is for:** what waits for you in each queue, and where the marketplace stands.

**How to open it:** click **Overview** in the sidebar. It is the first page after you sign in.

- **Waiting for review** shows a tile for each queue your role works on, for example employer registrations, agency licences, institute accreditation, agency mandates, job Truth Cards, worker documents, SkillShows, trust reports, lead follow-ups due or overdue invoices. Click a tile to open the queue.
- **Marketplace** shows workers registered, profiles complete, workers findable by employers, verified employers, agencies and institutes, live jobs, applications in the last 30 days, offers accepted, mobility cases open and workers joined.
- A warning shows how many destination corridors still need legal review.
