## Messages

**What it is for:** talk to workers who applied to your jobs. Workers' phone numbers stay hidden.

**How to open it:** click **Messages** in the sidebar. A number shows unread conversations. You can also open an applicant and click **Message the worker**.

1. Filter by **Job** if you like, and open a conversation.
2. Type in **Write a message** (up to 2,000 characters).
3. To add files, click **Attach**: up to 3 files, PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each.
4. Click **Send**.

A conversation closes 30 days after the application ended. Never ask a worker for money. To report a worker's message, click **Report** on it.
