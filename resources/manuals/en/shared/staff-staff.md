## Staff

**What it is for:** the BLUESKILLS team members and what each can do.

**How to open it:** click **Staff** in the sidebar. The list shows each person's role and whether their **Two-factor** is **On** or **Not set up**.

### Invite a staff member

1. Under **Invite a staff member**, enter the **Work email** and choose the **Role**: Superuser, Verification Officer, Trust Officer, CRM Agent or Finance Officer.
2. Click **Send invitation**. The link works for 3 days. Cancel it with **Cancel invitation** while it is pending.

### Change a role

Choose a new role in **Role for …**.

### Deactivate a staff member

Click **Deactivate** and confirm. They can no longer sign in; their past actions stay in the audit log.

Rules: BLUESKILLS needs at least one Superuser, you can't deactivate yourself, and an email that already belongs to a BLUESKILLS account can't be invited.
