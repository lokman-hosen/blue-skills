## Configuration

**What it is for:** the countries workers come from and go to, each corridor's rules, and the languages offered. (A corridor is a route from a source country to a destination country.) Every change is logged.

**How to open it:** click **Configuration** in the sidebar.

### Change a country

1. Click a country in **Countries**.
2. Under **Settings**, set the **Currency (ISO code)**, and tick **Source** (workers can come from it), **Destination** (jobs can be posted to it) and **Legal review** (the corridor rules had legal review). Save.

### Change the corridor rules

On the country page, under **Corridor rules**:

- **Which agencies may recruit**, the **Worker cost policy** and the **Notice shown to workers** (text).
- **Languages**.
- **Costs a worker may lawfully pay**: jobs that make workers pay anything else can't be submitted.
- **What every job must disclose**, for example salary or working hours.
- **Documents a mobility case asks for**.
- **Mobility case stages**: click **Add stage**, **Move up**, **Move down** or **Remove stage**. Tick **Done when the case opens** for a stage that accepting the offer already completes. Up to 20 stages.

Click **Save rules**. New jobs and cases use them; cases already open keep their stages.

### Languages

Under **Languages**, set each language to **Offered to users**, **In review** or **Planned**.
