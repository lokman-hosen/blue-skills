## Billing

**What it is for:** see your organization's BLUESKILLS plan, posting credits, seats and invoices. Workers never pay BLUESKILLS, and this is separate from any cost a worker pays for a job.

**How to open it:** click **Billing** in the sidebar.

- **Plan** shows your plan, since when, and what it includes (posting credits per period, seats, talent search, recruitment projects).
- **Posting credits:** one credit publishes one job. **Credit history** lists every credit added or used.
- **Seats:** the number of team members your plan includes.
- **Invoices:** click an invoice to open it. **Waiting for payment** and **Overdue** invoices still need to be paid. Click **Print** on an invoice to print it or save it as a PDF.

To pay an invoice, pay by bank transfer and quote the invoice number. Your BLUESKILLS contact sends the bank details. BLUESKILLS records the payment by hand, so the status changes to **Paid** after the finance team has checked it. Posting credits on an invoice are added when it is paid. To change your plan, contact BLUESKILLS.

If you see **Plan limits aren't enforced yet**, nothing is blocked: credits and seats are only counted for now. Once limits are switched on, you may see messages such as **You have no posting credits left** or that all your seats are taken. Ask BLUESKILLS for more credits, more seats or another plan.
