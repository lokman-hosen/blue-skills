## Agency mandate requests

**What it is for:** a recruitment agency must have your written mandate before it can post jobs for you on BLUESKILLS. (A mandate is your permission for the agency to recruit for you, for certain trades, a destination and a number of workers.) You confirm or decline each request.

**How to open it:** when an agency asks, the Dashboard shows **… agency mandate requests are waiting for your answer**. Click it. You are also told by notification.

1. Open the request. Check the agency, the **Trades**, the destination, the number of **Workers** and the dates.
2. If you have really engaged this agency for this work, add a **Note to the agency** if you like and click **Confirm mandate**. BLUESKILLS then checks the signed letter and the agency's licence.
3. If not, click **Decline**.

Confirm only agencies you have actually engaged. Workers see the agency's name on the jobs it posts for you.
