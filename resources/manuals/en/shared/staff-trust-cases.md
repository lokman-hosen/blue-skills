## Trust & safety

**What it is for:** investigate trust cases with evidence, suspend and restore jobs, organizations or workers, and decide appeals.

**How to open it:** click **Trust & safety** in the sidebar. Choose **Active**, **Mine**, **Closed** or **All**, and click a case.

### Handle a case

1. Under **Handling**, choose who handles it and the **Priority**. Click **Start investigating** when you begin.
2. Under **Evidence**, click **Add evidence**. Write what you found and attach screenshots, call logs or payment records (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 files). Click **Save evidence**.

### Suspend or lift a suspension

1. Under **Actions and appeals**, click **Suspend**.
2. Choose the **Reason** and write a note. The note is required.
3. Confirm.

What happens:

- **A job** leaves search and its applicants are told. Existing applications carry on. Only live or paused jobs can be suspended; reject jobs still in review from **Job reviews**.
- **An organization:** all its live jobs are suspended and applicants told. Members can sign in to read and appeal but can't change anything. Only verified organizations can be suspended.
- **A worker** is hidden from employers and can't apply, but can sign in and appeal.

Click **Lift suspension** to undo it. Everything suspended with it comes back and the owner is told.

### Decide an appeal

Appeals appear under **Actions and appeals**.

1. Read the appeal.
2. Click **Lift suspension** to accept it, or **Keep suspension**.
3. Write a note. The person who appealed sees it.

You can't decide an appeal against an action you took yourself; another trust officer must.

### Close a case

1. Click **Close case**.
2. Choose the reason and add a note.
3. Confirm. Linked reports close too. Reporters are told only whether action was taken.
