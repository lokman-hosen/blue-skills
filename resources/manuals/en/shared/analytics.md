## Analytics

**What it is for:** see how hiring is going: applications, each stage up to joining, and how long it takes. It shows counts only, never scores of people.

**How to open it:** click **Analytics** in the sidebar.

1. Choose the period in **From** and **To**, then click **Show**.
2. Read the numbers:
   - **Live jobs** and **Applications in this period**.
   - **Time to shortlist** and **Time to offer**: the middle value, in days, from applying.
   - **Stages:** how many applications reached each stage, counting everyone who reached it or went further.
   - **Where applicants come from:** applied themselves, invited by you, or withdrew.
   - **By job** and, for employers, **Active projects**.

If you see **No applications in this period**, choose a longer period.
