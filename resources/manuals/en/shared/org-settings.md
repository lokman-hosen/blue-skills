## Settings

Settings hold your own account details. Click **Settings** in the sidebar, or click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and then **Settings**.

### Profile

1. Click **Profile**.
2. Change your name or email address.
3. Click **Save**. You see **Profile updated.**

If you change your email address, BLUESKILLS sends a link to the new address. Click it to confirm the address.

### Password, two-factor authentication and passkeys

Click **Security**. You may be asked to confirm your password first.

**Change your password:** see "Change your password" in Getting started.

**Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA):**

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Turn on 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app, or type the **Setup key** into the app.
4. Click **Continue setup** and enter the 6-digit code the app shows. Click **Confirm**.
5. Click **Show recovery codes** and save them somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Each code works once if you lose your phone. Click **Make new codes** when you run low.

To turn it off, click **Turn off 2FA**.

**Add a passkey:** click **Add a passkey**, give it a name such as "work laptop" and click **Save passkey**. Follow what your device asks. To remove one, click **Remove passkey**.

### Appearance

Click **Appearance** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.

### Notification settings

Open the bell in the top bar and click **Notification settings**. Choose which notifications also come by email, then save. Emails go to the address you sign in with.

### Delete your account

1. In **Profile**, find **Delete account** and click it.
2. Enter your password to confirm.

You can't sign in again with this account. If you are an owner, make sure someone else in your organization is also an owner before you delete your account.
