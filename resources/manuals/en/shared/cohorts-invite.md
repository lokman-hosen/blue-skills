### Invite graduates

Open an open cohort. Under **Invite graduates** you find three ways to share the invitation. BLUESKILLS doesn't message anyone for you.

- **Invite link:** click **Copy invite link** and send it.
- **WhatsApp message:** click **Copy WhatsApp message** and paste it into WhatsApp.
- **QR code:** click **Download QR code** for posters and classroom slides.

Graduates open the link, sign in with their phone and choose **Accept and join** or **No, thanks**. They appear in the cohort once they accept.

### Add a roster (optional)

A roster is your own list of graduates, so you can see who has joined. Nobody on it is contacted.

1. In **Roster (optional)**, type one graduate per line: name, comma, phone number. For example: `Rahim Uddin, +8801711000001`.
2. Click **Add to roster**. You can add up to 300 lines at a time.

If some lines are wrong, BLUESKILLS lists them: each needs a name and a valid phone, separated by a comma. To remove someone nobody has heard from, click the remove button next to them.
