## Talent pools

**What it is for:** group graduates who joined your cohorts, for example "Electricians ready to travel". Publish a pool to let verified employers and agencies browse it.

**How to open it:** click **Talent pools** in the sidebar.

### Create a pool

1. Click **New pool**.
2. Enter the **Pool name** and a **Short description**. Choose a trade if you like.
3. Save. You see **Pool created. Add graduates to it.**

### Add or remove graduates

1. Open the pool and click **Add graduates**.
2. Tick the graduates. Only graduates who accepted one of your cohorts and aren't in the pool yet are listed.
3. Click **Add**.

To take someone out, click the remove button next to their name.

### Publish a pool

Click **Publish to employers**. Verified employers and agencies can then browse it. They only see graduates who chose to be found by employers; the column **Visible to employers** tells you who. Click **Stop publishing** to make it private again.

Your accreditation must be verified by BLUESKILLS before you can publish.
