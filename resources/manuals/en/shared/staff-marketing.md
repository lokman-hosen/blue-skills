## Marketing

**What it is for:** campaigns with their own link and QR code, and a funnel showing how many visitors signed up, finished their profile, applied and were deployed.

**How to open it:** click **Marketing** in the sidebar.

### Read the funnel

Choose a period with **From** and **To** and click **Show**. The funnel shows Visitors, Leads, Signed up, Profile complete, Qualified, Applied, Selected and Deployed, each with the percentage of the step before. Visitors are counted per day; the other steps follow the people who arrived in the period, as they are today.

### Create a campaign

1. Click **New campaign**.
2. Enter the **Name** and choose the **Channel** (for example job fair, social media or partner).
3. Choose the **Country** and the **Partner institute or agency** if there is one.
4. Choose **Where the link goes**: the sign-up page or job search.
5. Choose **Starts** and, if you know it, **Ends (optional)**.
6. Click **Create campaign**.

The link code is made from the name and never changes, so printed QR codes keep working.

### Share and manage a campaign

Open a campaign to:

- click **Copy link** or **Download QR code**;
- see **Visitors per day** and its own funnel;
- click **Edit** to change its details;
- click **Pause** or **Resume**;
- click **End campaign** and confirm. The link keeps working but no longer counts visitors or credits sign-ups. This can't be undone.
