## Dashboard

**What it is for:** a quick view of your institute's graduates on BLUESKILLS.

**How to open it:** click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It is the first page after you sign in.

- **Graduates joined**, and how many on your rosters are still waiting.
- **With a SkillShow:** graduates who added a work video.
- **Training attested** and **Assessments passed**.
- **Cohorts** and how many are open, with your recent cohorts. Click **All cohorts** to see them all.
