### Applicants

Click **Applicants** on a job to see who applied.

- **Pipeline stages** at the top filter the list by stage.
- Each row shows the worker, **Requirements** met (for example "4 of 5"), the stage and when they applied.
- Click an applicant to see the **Skill Passport at application** (a dated copy that later edits don't change), the documents the worker shared and the **History**.

Your role can't move applicants, reject them or make offers. Ask a colleague whose role can.
