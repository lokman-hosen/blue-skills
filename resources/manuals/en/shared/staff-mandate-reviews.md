## Mandates

**What it is for:** check each agency mandate after the employer confirmed it. (A mandate is an employer's written permission for an agency to recruit for it.) Only verified mandates let an agency post jobs.

**How to open it:** click **Mandates** in the sidebar. Mandates confirmed by employers are listed oldest first.

1. Click a mandate.
2. Read the signed letter and compare it with the trades, destination, number of workers and dates.
3. Check the licence line: **Licence valid for this destination**, or **Licence expired or not valid for this destination**.
4. Decide.

Mandate reasons include a letter not signed by the employer, a letter that doesn't match the trades, destination or headcount, and an expired or out-of-scope licence.
