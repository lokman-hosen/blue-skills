## Shortlists

**What it is for:** keep lists of workers your team is considering. A list gives nobody extra access to a worker's profile, and workers are not told.

**How to open it:** click **Shortlists** in the sidebar.

### Create a shortlist

1. Click **New shortlist**.
2. Enter a **Name**, for example "Electricians for Dubai towers".
3. Choose a job in **For job**, or **Not for a specific job**.
4. Click **Create list**.

### Add and remove workers

- Workers are added from their profile in **Talent** (**Add to shortlist**).
- Open a list and click **Remove** next to a worker to take them off it.
- If a worker is marked as no longer visible, they changed their privacy settings and you can't see their profile.

### Delete a shortlist

Open the list, click **Delete list** and confirm. The list and its notes are deleted. Workers are not told.
