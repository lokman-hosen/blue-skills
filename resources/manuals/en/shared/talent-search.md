## Talent

**What it is for:** find workers who chose to be found by employers and agencies, and invite them to apply.

**How to open it:** click **Talent** in the sidebar. Talent search opens after BLUESKILLS has verified your organization.

### Search for workers

1. Type a name, skill or Skill Passport number in the search box.
2. Use the filters: trade, where they live, where they want to work, **Minimum years of experience**, language, and **Show only** (**Passport ready**, **Available within 30 days**, **Has issuer-verified evidence**, **Has a SkillShow**).
3. To see how well workers fit a job, choose it in **Compare with a job**.
4. Click a worker to open their Skill Passport.

Phone numbers and documents never appear here.

### Invite a worker to apply

1. Open the worker's profile and click **Invite to apply**.
2. Choose one of your open jobs.
3. Add a short note (up to 300 characters) and click **Send invitation**.

The worker sees the job's Truth Card and your note. You can write to them freely once they apply. You can't invite a worker twice to the same job, or invite someone who already applied. Your organization can send a limited number of invitations each day.

### Add a worker to a shortlist

1. Open the worker's profile.
2. Under **Add to shortlist**, choose a list and add a note for your team if you like.
3. Click **Add**.
