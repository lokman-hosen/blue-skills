## Team

**What it is for:** decide who can work in your organization on BLUESKILLS and what each person can do.

**How to open it:** click **Team** in the sidebar.

Each person has one role. The role decides which pages and buttons they see. See the manual for each role to learn what it can do.

### Invite someone

1. Under **Invite a team member**, enter their **Work email**.
2. Choose their **Role**.
3. Click **Send invitation**. You see **Invitation sent.**

They get an email with a link. The invitation works for 7 days and is listed under **Pending invitations** until they accept it. To stop it, click **Cancel invitation**.

### Change someone's role

1. Find the person in the list.
2. Choose a new role in **Role for …**. You see **Role updated.**

### Remove someone

1. Find the person and click **Remove**.
2. Confirm. They lose access to your organization straight away. Their own BLUESKILLS account is not deleted.

### Rules to know

- Only roles for your kind of organization can be chosen.
- An organization always needs at least one owner. To change or remove the last owner, first make someone else an owner.
- You can't invite someone who is already a member.
