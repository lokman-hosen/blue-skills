## Trust reports

**What it is for:** see what workers and organizations reported through **Report this job** and **Report message**. Dismiss each report with a reason, or handle it on a case.

**How to open it:** click **Trust reports** in the sidebar. Choose **Waiting** or **All**.

### Dismiss a report

1. Click **Dismiss** on the report.
2. Choose a reason: not enough evidence, not against the rules, duplicate of another report, or resolved with the parties. Add a note if you like.
3. Confirm. The reporter is told it was reviewed and no action was needed.

### Open a case

1. Click **Open case** on the report.
2. Choose the **Priority** (Low, Normal, High or Urgent) and write a short **Summary**.
3. Confirm. If a case about the same job, organization or worker is already open, the report joins it.
