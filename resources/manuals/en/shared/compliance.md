## Compliance

**What it is for:** check in one place that your agency can keep recruiting: your licence, the mandates behind your job orders, and mobility cases that need attention.

**How to open it:** click **Compliance** in the sidebar.

- **Licence:** **Valid**, **Ends soon: renew it before jobs pause**, or **Not valid: job orders can't go live**. Click **Open licence** to see it.
- **Job orders without a usable mandate:** these jobs are paused, or will be paused tonight, because their mandate ended, was revoked, or your licence no longer covers the destination. Get a new mandate, then resume them.
- **Mandates:** each verified mandate with its **Valid until** date, **Places** left and **Live jobs**. **Ends within 30 days** warns you early. **Can't back jobs now** means no new jobs can use it.
- **Mobility cases:** cases with documents to check, overdue next steps or on hold.
