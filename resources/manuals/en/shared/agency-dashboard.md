## Dashboard

**What it is for:** a quick view of your agency's work.

**How to open it:** click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It is the first page after you sign in.

- **Licence warnings:** if your licence ends soon you see **Your agency licence ends on …**; if it isn't valid you see **Your agency licence isn't valid. Job orders can't go live until it is renewed.**
- **Jobs:** how many are live, in review, need changes or are drafts, and your recent job orders.
- **Applicants:** the total, new, shortlisted and upcoming interviews.
- **Mobility:** open cases, documents to check, overdue next steps and cases on hold.

Click a number to open the matching list. You only see the numbers your role is allowed to see.
