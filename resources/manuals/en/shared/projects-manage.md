## Projects

**What it is for:** follow a bulk hire across several jobs, for example 200 electricians for one site, with a target headcount and every stage up to joining.

**How to open it:** click **Projects** in the sidebar.

### Create a project

1. Click **New project**.
2. Enter a **Project name**, for example "200 Electricians — Dubai", and the **Target headcount** (at least 1).
3. Add the **Target date (optional)**, the **Destination** and the **Source countries**.
4. Under **Jobs in this project**, tick the jobs it covers. A job can be in only one project at a time.
5. Under **Team**, tick the people working on it.
6. Click **Create project**.

Jobs and applicants stay as they are; the project only groups them.

### Follow a project

Open a project to see **Joined of target**, the number of people at each stage (Applied, Qualified, Interview, Selected, Visa and travel, Joined), its jobs, its team and all its applicants.

### Reject many applicants at once

1. In **Applicants across the project**, tick the applicants.
2. Choose the **Reason workers see**.
3. Click **Reject selected** and confirm.

Each worker is told the reason. Applicants who already accepted an offer or withdrew are skipped.

### Edit, complete, cancel or reopen

- Click **Edit project** to change its details, jobs or team.
- Click **Mark completed** when the hire is done. Click **Reopen** to open it again.
- Click **Cancel project** to stop it. The jobs and applicants stay as they are. A cancelled project can't be reopened.
