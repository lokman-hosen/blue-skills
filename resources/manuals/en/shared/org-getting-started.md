## Getting started

BLUESKILLS works in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. You don't need to install anything.

### Join your organization

Most people join an organization that is already on BLUESKILLS. Someone in your organization sends you an invitation by email.

1. Open the email **You are invited to join … on BLUESKILLS** and click **Accept invitation**.
2. If you are new to BLUESKILLS, choose **Create an account to accept**. Enter your name, your work email and a password.
3. If you already have an account, choose **I already have an account** and sign in.
4. Check your email for a message from BLUESKILLS and click the link in it to confirm your email address.
5. If BLUESKILLS shows **You have been invited**, click **Accept**.

An invitation works for 7 days. If yours has expired, ask your organization to send a new one.

### Sign in

1. Go to the BLUESKILLS sign-in page. It says **Sign in to BLUESKILLS**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Keep me signed in** only on your own device.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. If you turned on two-factor authentication, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. (Two-factor authentication means you need your password and a code from your phone. It keeps your account safe even if someone learns your password.)

You can also click **Sign in with a passkey** if you added a passkey. A passkey lets your phone or computer sign you in without a password.

**Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and click **Email me a reset link**. Open the email, click the link and choose a new password.

**Lost your phone with the authenticator app?** On the code screen click **Use a recovery code instead** and type one of the recovery codes you saved.

### The screen

- **The sidebar** on the side of the screen lists the pages you can use. Pages your role can't use are not shown. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top to open it.
- **The top bar** shows the bell for notifications, the language and the light or dark theme.
- **Your organization** is named in the top bar. If you belong to more than one organization, click it and choose another one to switch.
- **Your name** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it to open **Settings** or to **Sign out**.

Coloured notices sometimes appear at the top of a page. For example, they tell you when your organization is still being reviewed, or suggest turning on two-factor authentication. Read them: they say what to do next.

### Notifications

1. Click the bell in the top bar. A number on the bell means you have unread notifications.
2. Click a notification to open the page it is about.
3. Click **Mark all as read** to clear the number.

To choose which notifications also come by email, click **Notification settings**. Notifications always appear in the app as well.

### Change the language or theme

- Click the language button in the top bar and choose your language.
- Click the theme button to choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (match your device).

### Sign out

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Click **Sign out**.

Always sign out on a shared or public computer.

### Change your password

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Security**. You may be asked to type your password again first.
3. Under **Change password**, enter your **Current password**, your **New password** and the new password again in **Confirm password**.
4. Click **Save**. You see **Password updated.**

Use a long password that you don't use anywhere else. On the live site a password needs at least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols.
