## Occupations

**What it is for:** the list of trades and skills: Sector → Occupation → Specialization → Skill. Workers pick from it and jobs require from it.

**How to open it:** click **Occupations** in the sidebar.

- Click **Add sector** or **Add occupation**. Open an occupation to **Add specialization** or **Add skill**.
- Every item needs an English name. Names in other languages are optional; until one is added, English shows. Items show **no … name** when a language is missing.
- Click **Rename** to change names.
- Click **Remove** to delete an item. Items in use can't be removed; rename them instead.
- Two items can't have the same English name.
