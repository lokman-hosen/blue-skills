### Record an assessment

Record the result of a trade test you ran.

1. Find the graduate and click **Record assessment**.
2. Enter the **Test** name, the **Result**, the **Score (0–100)** if there is one, the **Date** (today or earlier) and a **Note** if you like.
3. Click **Save result**. You see **Result saved. The graduate has been told.**

A pass appears on the Skill Passport as **Issuer verified**. A result that isn't a pass stays between you and the graduate.
