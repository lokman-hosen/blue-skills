## CRM

**What it is for:** follow up with workers: where they came from, how far they have got, and when to call next.

**How to open it:** click **CRM** in the sidebar.

### Find people

- Choose a view: **All**, **Due today**, **Mine** or **Unassigned**.
- Filter by **Name**, **Follow-up** status, **Stage**, **Source** or **Campaign**.

Workers who sign up appear here by themselves.

### Add a lead

Someone you met at a job fair, on the phone or through a referral.

1. Click **Add a lead**.
2. Enter the **Full name**, **Country** and **Mobile number**.
3. Choose **How we met them**: walk-in, call centre, referral or campaign. For a campaign, choose the **Campaign**.
4. Tick **Assign to me** if you will follow up.
5. Save. If they sign up later with this number, the lead is linked to their account.

You can't add the same number twice; open the existing lead instead.

### Work on a lead

1. Click a lead. Opening the page, which shows the phone number, is recorded in the audit log.
2. **Progress** shows how far the worker has really got: signed up, profile complete, qualified, applied, selected, deployed.
3. To record a call or message, use **Log a contact**: choose **How** (phone call, WhatsApp, SMS, email or in person), the **Result**, a **Note** and the next follow-up date. Click **Save contact**.
4. Under **Follow-up**, change who it is assigned to, the follow-up status and the next follow-up date, and save.

Respect the worker's choice: if you see **This worker turned off calls from BLUESKILLS**, don't contact them. A lead marked **Do not contact** can't be contacted until its status is changed, and only do that if the person asked you to call again.
