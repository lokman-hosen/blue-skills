### Attest a graduate's training

Attesting confirms a graduate completed the course. It appears on their Skill Passport as **Issuer verified**, naming your institute.

1. Find the graduate in **Graduates** or on the cohort page. They must have joined (accepted the invitation).
2. Click **Attest completion**.
3. Choose **Completed on** (today or earlier) and add a **Note** if you like.
4. Save. You see **Training attested. The graduate has been told.**

A graduate can have one attestation per cohort.
