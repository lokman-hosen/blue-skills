## Mobility

**What it is for:** follow each hired worker's permit, visa, travel and joining. A mobility case opens by itself when a worker accepts an offer.

**How to open it:** click **Mobility** in the sidebar.

- The list shows open cases with the current stage, progress and next due date.
- Filter by **Case status** and by **Documents to check**, **Overdue next steps** or **On hold**. Search by worker or case number.
- Open a case to see every stage, the next action for the worker, the documents and the history.

Your role can follow cases but can't change them.
