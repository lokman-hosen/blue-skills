## Roles & permissions

**What it is for:** decide what each role can do in BLUESKILLS. Only you, the Superuser, can see and change this.

**How to open it:** click **Roles & permissions** in the sidebar.

### Read the list

Roles are grouped as BLUESKILLS staff, employer roles, agency roles and institute roles. For each role you see how many **People** hold it and how many **Permissions** it has, for example "4 of 14". **Changed** means the role differs from how BLUESKILLS set it up. The Superuser row is **Locked**.

### Change a role's permissions

1. Click **Edit permissions** on the role.
2. Permissions are grouped by area and module. Each has a short explanation of what it allows.
3. Tick or untick the permissions. **Select all** or **Clear all** changes a whole module. A permission you changed from the default is marked **On by default** or **Off by default**.
4. Click **Save changes**. BLUESKILLS lists exactly what the role **Will now be able to** do and what it **Will no longer be able to** do.
5. Check the list and click **Save changes** again.

The change applies to everyone with that role, in every organization, from their next page. It is written to the audit log.

### Put a role back to its defaults

On a changed role, click **Reset to defaults** and confirm.

### What can't be changed

A lock and the reason are shown next to these:

- The Superuser always has every staff permission.
- Every staff role keeps **Use the staff workspace**.
- **Manage staff and read the audit log** is for the Superuser only, because it would let someone make themselves Superuser.
- Owners always keep **Manage the team**, so every organization has someone who can manage its members.
- Each role is only offered the permissions that fit it. For example, mandate permissions only appear for agency roles, and course permissions only for institute roles.

The user manuals describe each role's default permissions. If you change a role, tell the people who hold it, because their manual may no longer match.
