## Dashboard

**What it is for:** a quick view of your organization's hiring.

**How to open it:** click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It is the first page after you sign in.

- **Jobs:** how many are **Live jobs**, **In review**, **Need changes** and **Drafts**, and your **Recent jobs**. Click **All jobs** to open the list.
- **Applicants:** the total, **New, not reviewed**, **Shortlisted** and **Upcoming interviews**.
- **Mobility:** open cases, **Documents to check**, **Overdue next steps** and cases **On hold**.

Click a number to open the matching list. You only see the numbers your role is allowed to see.
