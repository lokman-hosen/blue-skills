## Register your organization

The first person from an organization registers it and becomes its owner.

1. On the sign-in page click **Register your organization**.
2. Enter your name, your work email and a password, then confirm your email address with the link BLUESKILLS sends.
3. Choose **What kind of organization are you?**: employer, recruitment agency or training institute.
4. Fill in the **Legal name**, the **Name shown to workers**, the **Country of registration**, the **City** and the **Registration number**. Agencies also enter the **Recruitment licence number** and **Licence valid until** (a future date).
5. If BLUESKILLS is in its pilot, enter the **Pilot invite code** your BLUESKILLS contact gave you. It starts with PILOT.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. On **Upload proof of registration**, click **Upload document** and add your trade licence, recruitment licence or accreditation certificate (PDF or image). Click **Go to my workspace** when you are done, or **Add documents later**.

BLUESKILLS checks every organization. Until it is approved you can prepare your workspace and write draft jobs, but you can't publish jobs or search talent.

### If BLUESKILLS asks for more or says no

You get an email and a notice in your workspace.

- **Our reviewer asked for more information:** click **Review documents** in the notice and upload what was asked for.
- **Your registration was not approved:** read the reason, upload corrected documents and click **Send for review again**.
