### What institutes can and can't do

- Graduates join a cohort only if they accept your invitation. They own their profile.
- You see a graduate's name, Skill Passport number, whether they have a SkillShow, and the training and tests you confirmed.
- You can attest training and record assessments. You can never edit a graduate's work history, or see their documents or phone number.
- You can attest training, record assessments and publish pools only after BLUESKILLS has verified your accreditation.
