## Courses

**What it is for:** list the courses your institute runs. Each course has cohorts (intakes), and each cohort has its own invite link for graduates.

**How to open it:** click **Courses** in the sidebar.

@include shared/institute-rules

### Add a course

1. Click **Add course**.
2. Enter the **Course title**. Add the **Trade**, the **Length in weeks** and a **Description** if you like.
3. Save. You see **Course added.**

### Edit a course

Click **Edit** on the course, change the details and save. Attestations already issued keep the course title they were issued with.

### Add a cohort to a course

1. Click **Add cohort** on the course.
2. Enter the **Cohort name**, for example "2026 Cohort", and the **Starts** and **Ends** dates if you know them. The end date can't be before the start date.
3. Save. You see **Cohort added. Share its link with your graduates.**
