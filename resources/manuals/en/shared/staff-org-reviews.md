## Verifications

**What it is for:** check every employer, agency and institute that registers, before it can publish jobs or search talent.

**How to open it:** click **Verifications** in the sidebar. Registrations waiting are listed oldest first, with their type, location, documents and any **Check flags**.

### Review a registration

1. Click a registration.
2. Compare the **Registration details** (legal name, registration number and, for agencies, the licence number and expiry) with the uploaded documents. Documents open in a new tab.
3. Decide.

@include shared/staff-decision-form

Reasons include, for example, a document that can't be read or has expired, details that don't match the document, a business that can't be confirmed, or a licence that doesn't cover recruitment. If no documents were uploaded, ask for more information. After **Ask for more**, the review shows **Waiting for the organization to send what was asked for.**

### Pilot invite codes

During the pilot, organizations need a code to register. Open **Pilot invite codes** from the Verifications page.

1. Under **Issue a code**, enter the **Partner** name, the organization type (or **Any type**), the number of **Uses** (1 to 50) and **Expires on (optional)**.
2. Click **Issue code**. Give the code to the partner yourself; BLUESKILLS doesn't send it.
3. To stop a code, click **Revoke** and confirm. Organizations that already used it keep their registration.

Every organization that registers with a code is still reviewed as usual.
