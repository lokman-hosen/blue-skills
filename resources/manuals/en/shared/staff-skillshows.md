## SkillShow moderation

**What it is for:** check every worker video (SkillShow) before anyone else sees it. Publish only real demonstrations of work.

**How to open it:** click **SkillShow moderation** in the sidebar. Videos are listed oldest first, after the automatic length and format check.

1. Play the video.
2. Click **Publish** if it shows the worker doing their work safely, without personal details.
3. Otherwise choose a **Reason if not publishing** and click **Reject**. Reasons: not a work demonstration, unsafe work, personal details shown (phone number or documents), poor quality, or content that breaks the rules. The worker sees the reason.
