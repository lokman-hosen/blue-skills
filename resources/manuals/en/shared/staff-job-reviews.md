## Job reviews

**What it is for:** check each job's Truth Card exactly as workers will see it, before it is published. Jobs that changed their pay, costs or conditions after publishing come back here too.

**How to open it:** click **Job reviews** in the sidebar. Jobs are listed oldest first. **Changed after publishing** marks jobs that came back.

### Review a job

1. Click a job.
2. Read the Truth Card. Under **Checks**:
   - **Rules met** means every required field for the destination is filled in and every worker cost is allowed. Otherwise you see how many problems there are.
   - **Employer verification** shows whether the employer is verified.
   - For agency jobs, check the mandate.
3. Decide.

Reasons for jobs include worker costs that aren't allowed on the corridor, unclear conditions, a salary that looks wrong, a duplicate job and prohibited or discriminatory content.
