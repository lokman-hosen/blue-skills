## Worker documents

**What it is for:** check the passport and ID scans, certificates and work references that workers upload. Each one is verified on its own.

**How to open it:** click **Worker documents** in the sidebar. Documents waiting are listed oldest first.

1. Click a document.
2. Click **Start review**. The document is assigned to you and the worker sees **Verification pending**. The file opens only after this.
3. Open the file and compare it with what the worker entered.
4. Choose **Approve** to mark it issuer verified, or **Reject** with a reason (for example unreadable, expired, altered or not the document described).
5. Save. The worker is told.

There is no "ask for more" for worker documents: to get a new upload, reject with a reason. Every time staff open a worker's file it is written to the audit log.
