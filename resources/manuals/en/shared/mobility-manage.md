## Mobility

**What it is for:** follow each hired worker's permit, visa, travel and joining. A mobility case opens by itself when a worker accepts an offer. The stages come from the destination country's rules.

**How to open it:** click **Mobility** in the sidebar.

### Find a case

- The list shows open cases you run or follow, with the current stage, progress and the next due date.
- Filter by **Case status** (Open, Closed, All) and by what needs attention: **Documents to check**, **Overdue next steps** or **On hold**.
- Search by worker or case number.

Some cases are run by another organization, for example an agency, and you only follow them. Those show **You follow this case** and can't be changed by you.

### Move a case forward

Only the current stage can be changed. Open the case and, on the current stage:

- Click **Mark done** when the stage is finished. The next stage starts.
- Click **Skip stage** if it isn't needed. Write the reason; the worker sees it.
- Click **Put on hold** if something blocks it. Write the reason; the worker sees it. Click **Resume** when it can go on.

### Tell the worker what to do next

1. In **What the worker should do next**, write the next step, for example "Book a medical test at an approved centre, then upload the receipt."
2. Add a **Due date** if there is one.
3. Click **Save next action**. The worker is told.

### Documents

The case has a checklist of documents.

- **Ask for a document:** click **Ask for a document**, choose the type, the name shown on the checklist, **Who provides it**, a due date and instructions. Click **Add to checklist**.
- **Check a worker's document:** open it, then click **Accept** or **Send back**. To send back, choose a reason (for example hard to read, expired or details don't match the passport). The worker sees the reason and uploads a new file.
- **Upload a document from your side,** for example the contract, work permit or visa: click **Upload** on the item and choose the file (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB). The worker always sees files you add. When you upload the contract, the worker is asked to confirm it or ask for changes.

### Close a case

1. Click **Close case**.
2. Choose **Why is the case closing?**: joined the job, worker withdrew, employer cancelled, visa refused, medical test not passed or another reason (write a note for "Other reason").
3. Confirm.

Closing can't be undone and the worker is told why. To close a case as **Joined the job**, every stage must be done or skipped first.
