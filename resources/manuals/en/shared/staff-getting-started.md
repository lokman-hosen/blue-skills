## Getting started

You use the BLUESKILLS staff workspace, **BLUESKILLS Operations**, in a web browser. Every staff account must use two-factor authentication. (Two-factor authentication means you need your password and a code from your phone to sign in.)

### Set up your account

A Superuser invites you by email.

1. Open the email **You are invited to the BLUESKILLS staff team** and click **Set up my account**. The link works for 3 days.
2. Enter your full name and choose a password. Click **Create my staff account**.
3. BLUESKILLS asks you to set up two-factor authentication straight away:
   1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
   2. Scan the QR code with the app, or type the **Setup key** into it.
   3. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and click **Confirm**.
   4. Click **Show recovery codes** and save them somewhere safe. Each code works once if you lose your phone.

Until two-factor authentication is set up, staff pages stay closed and you see **Set up two-factor authentication to use staff tools.**

### Sign in

1. Go to the BLUESKILLS sign-in page and enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign in**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If you lost your phone, click **Use a recovery code instead**.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email.

### The screen

- **The sidebar** lists the pages your role can use. Pages for other staff roles are hidden. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top to open it.
- **Overview** is the first page. **Waiting for review** shows how many items wait in each queue you can work on; click a tile to open that queue. **Marketplace** shows the main numbers for the whole platform.
- **The top bar** shows the bell for notifications, the language and the theme.
- **Your name** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it to open **Settings** or **Sign out**.

Queues list the oldest item first. Work from the top.

### Everything you do is recorded

Every decision and change is written to the audit log with your name, the reason and the values before and after. Opening a worker's private file or phone number is recorded too. Only open what you need for the task.

### Sign out

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Sign out**. Always sign out on a shared computer.

### Change your password

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Security** and confirm your password if asked.
3. Under **Change password**, enter your **Current password**, your **New password** and the new password again.
4. Click **Save**.

On the live site a password needs at least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols.
