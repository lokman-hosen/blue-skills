## User Manual

This guide. Click **User Manual** in the sidebar to open it at any time.

- **Contents** lists every part of the manual. Click a heading to jump to it.
- **Search this manual** finds a word in the manual. Matching words are highlighted and other parts are hidden. Clear the box to see everything again.
- **Print** prints the whole manual, without the menu and buttons.
- If you have more than one role, choose **Manual for** to read the manual of your other role.
