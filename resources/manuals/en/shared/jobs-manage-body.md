### Job statuses

- **Draft:** you are still writing it. Workers can't see it.
- **Submitted** and **Under review:** BLUESKILLS is checking it, usually within two working days.
- **Changes requested:** the reviewer asked you to change something. Read the reason, edit the job and submit it again.
- **Published:** live. Workers can find it and apply.
- **Paused:** hidden from workers for now.
- **Closed:** no longer takes applications.
- **Archived:** put away. **Rejected:** not approved. **Suspended:** stopped by BLUESKILLS trust staff.

### Post a job

1. Click **Post a job**.
2. Fill in each step and click **Save and continue**:
   1. **Requirement:** **Job title** (for example "Building Electrician"), the trade, **Openings** (1 to 10,000), the **Destination country**, the **City**, and **Joining from** and **Joining by** dates.
   2. **Skills:** **Required skills** (up to 15), **Minimum years of experience** and up to 5 **Language requirements**.
   3. **Employment terms:** **Salary** with currency and period, **Hours per day**, **Days per week**, **Overtime terms** and **Contract length (months)**.
   4. **Mobility & costs:** accommodation, food, transport, medical and airfare (provided, allowance, deducted from pay or not provided), **Work permit paid by**, **Visa paid by**, the **Application deadline**, and **Costs and who pays**. Click **Add a cost** for each cost, even the ones the employer pays.
3. On **Preview & submit**, read **What workers will see**: the Job Truth Card exactly as workers see it.
4. If BLUESKILLS lists **Fix these before submitting**, fix each item first.
5. Click **Submit for review**. You see **Sent for review.** You are told the outcome by notification and email.

Rules to know:

- Leave a field empty if it isn't decided. Workers then see **Not disclosed**, never a guess. Some countries require some fields, for example salary or working hours; BLUESKILLS tells you which ones.
- Every cost a worker pays must be listed. Some costs can't be charged to workers in some countries. If you see **Workers cannot pay this for jobs in …**, change who pays.
- Your organization must be verified by BLUESKILLS before a job can be submitted. You can still write drafts.
- Dates can't be in the past.

### Edit a job

1. Open the job and click **Edit job**.
2. Change what you need and click **Save and continue** on each step.
3. On **Preview & submit**, click **Submit for review** if the job needs to be checked again.

You can't edit a job while it is being reviewed. If you change the pay, the employer, the worker costs or the conditions of a live job, it goes back to review and is hidden from workers until it is approved. Workers who already applied are told and asked to read the Truth Card again.

### Pause, resume, close or archive a job

Open the job and click:

- **Pause** to hide a live job for a while. **Resume** to show it again.
- **Close job** to stop taking applications.
- **Archive** to put away a closed job or a draft you don't need.

### Applicants

Click **Applicants** on a job (or the number of applicants in the list) to open its pipeline.

- **Pipeline stages** at the top filter by stage: Applied, Profile reviewed, Qualified, Shortlisted, Interview, Trade assessment, Selected, Offer and the closed stages.
- Each row shows the worker, **Requirements** met (for example "4 of 5"), the stage, who handles it and when they applied.

**Move applicants to another stage:**

1. Tick **Select** next to one or more applicants (up to 200 at a time).
2. Choose a stage in **Move to** and click **Move**.

Some moves are not possible, for example back to an earlier stage. You are told how many moved and how many could not.

**Open an applicant** to see:

- **Skill Passport at application:** the dated copy the worker sent. Later edits by the worker don't change it. Only what the worker still shares is shown.
- **Contact and documents:** the phone number and passport scan, only if the worker shared them.
- **History** of every stage.

**Assign who handles it:** choose a team member in **Handled by**.

**Book an interview:**

1. Click **Book interview**.
2. Choose the **Date and time** (in the future), the **Length (minutes)** (10 to 240) and **How**: in person, video call or phone call.
3. For an in-person interview, add the **Address**. For a call, add the **Call link or number**.
4. Add **Instructions for the worker** and save. The worker sees it in their application, in their own time zone.

After the interview, choose the **Outcome**: Passed, Not passed, Did not attend or Cancelled.

**Make an offer:**

1. Click **Make an offer**.
2. Enter the salary. It can't be lower than the salary on the Truth Card.
3. Add the start date, the date the worker must answer by, and a message.
4. Click **Send offer**. The worker answers in their application.

Only one offer can wait for an answer at a time. The job needs a salary before you can make an offer. When the worker accepts, a mobility case opens (see Mobility).

**Reject an applicant:**

1. Click **Reject**.
2. Choose a **Reason**. The worker sees it.
3. Add an **Internal note** if you like. Only your team sees it.
4. Confirm. Rejection ends the application.

**Message the worker:** click **Message the worker** (see Messages).
