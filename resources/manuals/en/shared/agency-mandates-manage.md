## Mandates

**What it is for:** a mandate is an employer's written permission for your agency to recruit for them: for certain trades, one destination, a number of workers and a period of time. You need a verified mandate before you can post jobs for that employer.

**How to open it:** click **Mandates** in the sidebar. The list shows each mandate, its employer, trades, workers, dates and status.

### How a mandate is approved

1. You request it and upload the signed letter. Status: **Waiting for the employer**.
2. The employer confirms it in BLUESKILLS. Status: **Verification pending** (BLUESKILLS checks your letter and licence).
3. BLUESKILLS approves it. Status: **Verified**. You can now post jobs inside it.

A mandate can also be **Declined** by the employer, **Rejected** by BLUESKILLS (with a reason), **Expired** or **Revoked**.

### Request a mandate

1. Click **Request a mandate**.
2. Choose the **Employer**. Only employers verified on BLUESKILLS can be chosen.
3. Choose the **Trades** (1 to 10), the destination and the number of **Workers** (1 to 10,000).
4. Add **Valid from** (optional) and **Valid until** (a future date).
5. Upload the **Signed mandate letter**.
6. Click **Send to the employer**. You see **Mandate request sent to the employer.**

Your licence must be valid and must cover the destination. Otherwise you see **Your licence is expired or does not cover this destination.** Fix it in **Licence** first.

### Add a document to a mandate

While a mandate waits for the employer or for BLUESKILLS, open it, click **Add a document**, choose the file and click **Upload**. Use this when BLUESKILLS asks for more.
