## Placements

**What it is for:** see employer interest and job applications of graduates who chose to share their progress with you.

**How to open it:** click **Placements** in the sidebar.

- The numbers at the top show how many graduates share their progress, **Invitations from employers**, how many **Applied to jobs** and how many **Joined a job**.
- Each graduate shows the employers who invited them and their applications.

Graduates choose to share their progress when they accept your invitation, or later in their Privacy page. If nobody shares yet, the page says so.
