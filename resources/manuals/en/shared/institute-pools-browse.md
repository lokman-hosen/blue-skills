## Institute pools

**What it is for:** browse graduates of verified training institutes, grouped by the institute. You only see graduates who chose to be found.

**How to open it:** click **Institute pools** in the sidebar.

1. Click a pool to see its graduates.
2. Click a graduate to open their Skill Passport. From there you can invite them to apply.

Training marked **Issuer verified** was confirmed by the institute itself.
