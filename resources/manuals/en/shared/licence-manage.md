## Licence

**What it is for:** your recruiting licence. Agencies need a valid licence and a verified mandate before they publish a job for an employer.

**How to open it:** click **Licence** in the sidebar.

You see the **Licence number**, when it **Expires** and whether it is valid or verified.

### Set the destinations your licence covers

1. Under **Destinations the licence covers**, choose every country your licence allows you to recruit for.
2. Save. You see **Licence destinations saved.**

BLUESKILLS checks these against your licence with each mandate. The licence number and expiry date come from your registration and are checked by BLUESKILLS. When you renew your licence, contact BLUESKILLS so the new details can be checked.
