## Suspensions and appeals

If BLUESKILLS suspends your organization or one of its jobs, you see a notice in your workspace. A suspended organization's jobs are hidden and changes are locked, but you can still sign in, read your workspace and appeal.

1. Click **See why and appeal** in the notice.
2. Read the reason.
3. Under **Your appeal**, explain what happened (at least 20 characters) and add facts that help, for example refunds or corrected terms.
4. Click **Send appeal**. Another trust officer reads it and you are told the decision.

Existing applications and mobility cases carry on while the appeal is reviewed.
