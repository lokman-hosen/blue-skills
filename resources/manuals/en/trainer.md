# Manual for trainers

You are a **Trainer** at a training institute on BLUESKILLS. You add courses and cohorts, keep cohorts open or closed, and confirm (attest) that graduates completed their training. Inviting graduates, assessments, talent pools and placements are done by other roles.

## Common questions

**I can't see the invite link on a cohort.** Inviting graduates is done by the institute owner or a placement officer.

**The Attest completion button is missing.** The graduate must have accepted the invitation, and BLUESKILLS must have verified your institute's accreditation.

**It says the graduate already has an attestation.** A graduate can have one attestation per cohort. To correct it, withdraw it and attest again.

**Can I change a graduate's work history?** No. Institutes can attest training but never edit a graduate's career history.
