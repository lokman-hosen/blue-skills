# Manual for recruiters

You are a **Recruiter** at an employer on BLUESKILLS. You post jobs, find and invite workers, move applicants through the pipeline, book interviews, make offers and follow hired workers' visas and travel. You can't manage the team or see billing.

## Common questions

**I can't submit a job for review.** Your organization must be verified first. Ask an owner. Also fix every item under **Fix these before submitting**.

**I can't edit a job.** You can't edit a job while BLUESKILLS reviews it. Wait for the decision.

**Why did my live job disappear from search?** You changed the pay, the worker costs or the conditions, so it went back to review. It comes back once it is approved.

**An applicant won't move to the stage I chose.** Some moves aren't allowed, for example back to an earlier stage or for a closed application. Refresh the page and try another stage.

**I can't make an offer.** Add a salary to the job first. The offer can't be lower than the Truth Card salary, and only one offer can wait for an answer at a time.

**I need someone added to the team.** Ask an owner or HR admin.
