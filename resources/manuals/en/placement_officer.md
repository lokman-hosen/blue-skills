# Manual for placement officers

You are a **Placement Officer** at a training institute on BLUESKILLS. You invite graduates to join cohorts, group them in talent pools that employers can browse, and follow their job applications and placements.

## Graduates

**What it is for:** everyone who joined your cohorts, with the training attested and the tests recorded for them.

**How to open it:** click **Graduates** in the sidebar.

Filter by **Graduate status** (Joined, Roster waiting, All) and by cohort. Attesting training and recording tests are done by trainers, assessors and the institute owner.

## Common questions

**I can't add a course or a cohort.** Courses and new cohorts are added by trainers and the institute owner. You can edit, close and reopen cohorts and invite graduates.

**I can't publish a pool.** BLUESKILLS must verify your institute's accreditation first.

**Employers see fewer graduates than are in my pool.** Employers only see graduates who chose to be found by employers. The **Visible to employers** column shows who.

**Placements is empty.** Graduates choose whether to share their progress with you. They can turn it on in their Privacy page.
