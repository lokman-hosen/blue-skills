# Manual for workers

BLUESKILLS helps you find honest overseas jobs. You build a free **Skill Passport** (your work profile), show your skills in a short video, apply for jobs and follow every step until you start work. BLUESKILLS is free for workers. Nobody from BLUESKILLS will ever ask you for money.

## Getting started

### Create your account

You only need a mobile phone number. There is no password.

1. Open BLUESKILLS and tap **Sign in**, then **Looking for work? Sign in with your phone**. You can also tap **Join free to apply** on any job, or open a link from a job fair or training institute.
2. Choose your language. You can change it any time.
3. Under **Your mobile number**, choose your **Country code** and type your **Mobile number**.
4. Tap **Send code on WhatsApp**. You get a 6-digit code on WhatsApp.
5. Type the code and tap **Verify**.
6. Under **Tell us about you**, type your **Full name** exactly as it is written on your passport, and choose the **Country you live in**.
7. Tap **Create my account**.

Next, BLUESKILLS asks two questions about your work: **Your trade** (the kind of work you do) and your **Years of experience**. Type 0 if you are just starting. Then choose **Build my profile by voice**, **Type my answers**, or **Save and continue later**.

Good to know:

- The code works for 10 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, wait one minute and tap **Send a new code**.
- You have 5 tries to type the code. After that, ask for a new code.
- If you ask for too many codes, wait one hour.
- Mobile numbers from Bangladesh, Nepal, India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka can be used.
- BLUESKILLS will never ask you for this code by phone. Never give it to anyone.

### Sign in again later

1. Tap **Sign in**, then **Looking for work? Sign in with your phone**, and enter the same mobile number.
2. Tap **Send code on WhatsApp**, then type the code and tap **Verify**.

### The screen

- **On a phone**, the menu is at the bottom of the screen: **Home**, **Jobs**, **Applications**, **Passport** and **More**. Tap **More** for SkillShow, Messages, Documents, Privacy, User Manual, Settings, the language, the theme and **Sign out**.
- **On a computer**, the same menu is on the side of the screen.
- **The bell** at the top shows your notifications. A number on it means you have something new.

### Home

**Home** is the first page. It shows:

- **Your Skill Passport** with the steps that are done and the ones still to do. Tap a step to do it.
- Invitations from training institutes to join their graduates.
- A warning if your account is suspended, with a link to see why and appeal.

### Notifications

Tap the bell to see your notifications. Tap one to open what it is about. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear them.

Important news (for example an interview, an offer or a new message) also comes on WhatsApp. If WhatsApp doesn't work, it comes by SMS. Nothing is sent at night between 10 pm and 7 am your time; it waits until morning. To choose which news comes outside the app, open the bell and tap **Notification settings**.

### Language and theme

Tap the language button to change the language. Tap the theme button to choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.

### Sign out

On a phone, tap **More**, then **Sign out** at the bottom. Always sign out on a shared phone.

You have no password, so there is no password to change. Your phone number is how you sign in.

## Home

See "Home" in Getting started. Come back to **Home** to see what to do next.

## Find jobs

**What it is for:** find jobs you can do. Every job is checked by BLUESKILLS before you see it.

**How to open it:** tap **Jobs**.

### Search for a job

1. Type a trade, city, skill or job code in the search box and tap **Search**.
2. To narrow the list, open **Filters**. Choose a country, a trade, and conditions such as **No costs for the worker**, **Accommodation provided** or **Salary shown**.
3. Use **Sort by** to see the **Newest first** or the jobs **Closing soonest**.
4. Tap a job to open it.

### Read the Job Truth Card

Every job has a **Truth Card**. It shows the pay, working hours, contract length, accommodation, food and every cost, with who pays each cost.

- **Not disclosed** means the employer did not give this information. Ask before you go further.
- **Worker-paid costs** lists everything you would pay. If it says **Nothing — no worker-paid costs**, you pay nothing.
- **X of Y requirements met** compares the job with your confirmed Skill Passport. It is only a guide, not a decision.

### Report a job

If something is wrong, for example someone asks you for money, tap **Report this job**.

1. Choose **What is wrong?**
2. Add details. Details are required if you choose **Something else**.
3. Tap **Send report**. The BLUESKILLS trust team looks at every report.

## Applications

**What it is for:** apply for jobs and follow each application.

**How to open it:** tap **Applications**.

### Apply for a job

You need your trade on your Skill Passport before you can apply.

1. Open a job in **Jobs** and read its Truth Card.
2. Tap **I can do this job**.
3. Under **What to share**, choose what this employer can see: your Skill Passport (always shared), your SkillShow video, your work history, your certificates, your passport scan and your phone number.
4. Tap **Confirm application**. You see **Application sent.**

A copy of your Skill Passport dated today goes with the application. If you change your Skill Passport later, the copy does not change.

Good to know:

- You can apply only once to each job.
- To share a passport scan, upload it first in **Documents**.
- If you don't share your phone number, the employer contacts you in the app.

### Follow an application

Open an application to see:

- its **stage**, for example Applied, Shortlisted, Interview or Offer;
- **Next:** what you should do now;
- your **Interviews**, with the date, time, place and instructions;
- **What you shared**.

### Invitations to apply

Employers and agencies can find your Skill Passport and invite you to apply. Invitations appear at the top of **Applications**.

- Tap **Read the Truth Card and apply** to read the job and apply.
- Tap **Not interested** to say no. The employer is told.

An invitation is not a job offer. Never pay anyone to apply.

### Answer an offer

When an employer makes you an offer, you see **You have an offer** in the application.

1. Read the pay, the **Start date** and **Answer by**.
2. Tap **Accept offer** or **Decline offer** and confirm.

If you accept, your mobility case opens (see "Your mobility case" below). If you decline, the application ends and you can't undo it. An offer can't pay less than the job's Truth Card.

### When a job changes after you applied

If the employer changes the pay, costs or conditions, BLUESKILLS checks the job again and you see **This job changed … after you applied**. Tap **Read the Truth Card**, then **I have read it**.

### Stop sharing an item

In **What you shared**, tap **Stop sharing** next to an item and confirm. The employer stops seeing it straight away. Your Skill Passport copy is part of every application and can't be removed.

### Withdraw an application

Tap **Withdraw application** and confirm. The employer is told you are no longer interested. You can't apply to that job again.

### Your mobility case

After you accept an offer, a **mobility case** follows your permit, visa, travel and joining. Tap **Open mobility case** in the application.

- **Your journey to …** shows every stage and where you are now.
- **Next action** says what you must do and by when.
- **History** shows everything that happened.

**Share a document:** the case lists the documents that are needed.

1. Find the document and choose **Share my scan on file**, or **Or upload a new file** (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB).
2. If an agency runs your case, tick whether the employer may also see it.
3. Tap **Upload and share**.

Share each document only when it is asked for. To stop, tap **Stop sharing**. If a document is **Sent back**, read the reason and share a new file.

**Check your contract:** when the contract arrives, read it. Tap **I confirm this contract** only if the pay, job and conditions match your offer. If something is wrong, tap **Ask for changes**, write what needs changing and tap **Send back**.

Your passport is only handed over for visa stamping and must come back to you right after. If anyone keeps your passport, report it.

## Skill Passport

**What it is for:** your work profile. You own it. Employers only see what you choose to share.

**How to open it:** tap **Passport**.

### Build it by voice or by typing (Voice-to-CV)

BLUESKILLS asks seven short questions: your work, years of experience, countries you worked in, main skills, passport, when you can start and where you want to work.

1. Tap **Update by voice** (or choose **Build my profile by voice** after sign-up).
2. Tap **Tap and speak** and answer. Tap **Stop** when you finish. Or tap **Type instead** and type your answer, then tap **Send answer**.
3. BLUESKILLS shows **What we heard** and **We understood:**. Check it.
4. Tap **Yes, correct** if it is right. Tap **Edit** or **Fill in myself** to fix it, or **Speak again**.
5. Do this for all seven questions, then tap **Review all answers**.
6. Check each answer and tap **Save to my Skill Passport**.

Nothing goes on your Skill Passport until you confirm it. Keep each recording under one minute and each typed answer under 500 letters. Your recording is deleted after you confirm the answer. If the microphone doesn't work, allow it in your browser settings or type your answers.

### Edit a section

Open your Skill Passport and tap the section you want to change:

- **About your work:** a **Headline** (for example "Industrial electrician"), your trade, specialization, years of experience (0 to 60) and the country you live in now.
- **Work history:** tap **Add work**. Enter the country, **Job title**, **Employer**, and how long (**Number of years** or **Exact dates**). Leave **Ended** empty if you still work there. You can edit or delete each job.
- **Skills:** tap the skills you can do. You can choose up to 30. Skills attested by your training institute can only be changed by the institute.
- **Languages:** tap **Add a language**, choose the language and your level. Up to 10 languages.
- **Passport, availability and pay:** your passport status and the year it is valid until, when you can start, up to five countries you want to work in (in order of preference), and your expected salary with its currency and period.
- **Profile photo:** optional. A clear photo of your face, JPG, PNG or WebP, at least 200 × 200 pixels and up to 5 MB.

Tap **Save** after each change.

### Add a certificate or reference

1. In **Certificates and references**, tap **Add a certificate or reference**.
2. Choose the **Type**: certificate, training record or work reference.
3. Type a **Name**, for example "Electrical training certificate".
4. Add **Expires on** if it has an end date. It must be in the future.
5. Choose the file (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB) and tap **Upload**.

Each document is checked by BLUESKILLS on its own. Until then it shows **Uploaded · unreviewed**. Later it shows **Verification pending**, **Issuer verified**, **Rejected** or **Expired**. To delete one, tap it and confirm.

Training and tests that an institute confirmed for you show as **Issuer verified**. You can't delete them, but you can tap **Hide from employers**.

## SkillShow

**What it is for:** a video of up to 30 seconds that shows you doing your work. It helps employers see your skill. It is shown as "worker-provided demonstration, not certification".

**How to open it:** tap **More**, then **SkillShow**.

### Add a SkillShow

1. Tap **Record or upload** and record a video, or choose one from your phone.
2. Tag the skills you show. Choose at least 1 and up to 6.
3. Under **Who can see it**, choose who can see the video.
4. Tap **Send for checking**.

Rules:

- At most 30 seconds and 50 MB. MP4, WebM or MOV.
- Show your hands and tools at work. Don't show documents or your phone number.
- Wear safety equipment. Record in good light with a steady phone.

BLUESKILLS checks every SkillShow before anyone else sees it, usually within two working days. Its status shows **Checking video**, **In moderation**, **Published** or **Not approved** with the reason.

### Change or delete your SkillShow

- Tap **Replace video** to send a new one. It is checked again.
- Change the skills or **Who can see it** and save.
- Tap **Delete SkillShow** and confirm. The video is deleted for good.

## Messages

**What it is for:** talk to the employers and agencies handling your applications. Your phone number stays hidden.

**How to open it:** tap **More**, then **Messages**. Or open an application and tap **Message the employer**.

### Send a message

1. Open a conversation.
2. Type in **Write a message** (up to 2,000 letters).
3. To add files, tap **Attach**. Up to 3 files, PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each.
4. Tap **Send**.

A conversation closes 30 days after the application ended.

### Report a message

Genuine employers never ask you for money. If someone does, or if they harass you:

1. Tap **Report** on the message.
2. Choose what happened. Add details if you choose "Something else".
3. Tap **Send report**. The other person is not told who reported it.

## Documents

**What it is for:** keep your passport and national ID scans. They never appear in search. You choose which applications can see them.

**How to open it:** tap **More**, then **Documents**.

### Upload a document

1. Under **Upload a document**, choose **Passport** or **National ID**.
2. Add the **Expiry date**. It must be in the future.
3. Choose the scan or photo (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB).
4. Tap **Upload**.

Uploading a new scan of the same type replaces the old one. Applications that had the old scan stop seeing it. BLUESKILLS staff check each scan before it shows as verified.

### Delete a document

Tap **Delete** and confirm. Every application that could see it stops seeing it straight away.

## Privacy

**What it is for:** choose who can find your Skill Passport, see what you shared, and delete your account.

**How to open it:** tap **More**, then **Privacy**.

### Who can find my Skill Passport

Choose one and it is saved:

- **Verified employers and agencies:** they can find you in talent search and invite you to apply.
- **Only jobs I apply to:** only employers of jobs you apply to see your profile.
- **Only me:** nobody can find you. Applications you already sent keep their copy.

Your phone number and passport scan never appear in search.

### What I have shared

A list of everything you shared, when, and whether it is **Active** or **Withdrawn**.

### Calls from BLUESKILLS

Tick **BLUESKILLS may contact me about my profile** if our team may call or message you to help. Employers never get your number from this.

### My institutes

If you joined a training institute's graduates, it is listed here.

- Tick **Let … follow my job applications and placement** to share your progress with the institute. Untick it to stop at once.
- Tap **Leave this institute** to leave. It no longer sees your profile.

An institute never sees your documents or phone number and can't change your work history.

### Delete my account

1. Tap **Send a code to …**. A code comes on WhatsApp.
2. Type the code and tap **Delete my account**.

Your Skill Passport, recordings, SkillShow and documents are deleted. Applications you already sent keep the copy you shared. This can't be undone, and the same number can't be used to sign up again.

## Settings

Tap **More**, then **Settings**.

- **Profile:** change your name and tap **Save**. Your phone number is your sign-in and can't be changed here.
- **Appearance:** choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.

To delete your account, use **Privacy**.

## Training institute invitations

A training institute may send you a link or QR code to join its graduates.

1. Open the link. It says which institute invites you and what it will see.
2. Sign in with your phone if asked.
3. Tick **Let … follow my job applications and placement** if you want to share your progress. It is off unless you tick it.
4. Tap **Accept and join**, or **No, thanks**.

If you join, the institute sees your name, Skill Passport number, whether you have a SkillShow, and the training and tests it confirms for you. It never sees your documents or phone number.

## Suspensions and appeals

If BLUESKILLS suspends your account, employers can't find you and you can't apply. You can still sign in. Home shows a warning with a link.

1. Open the link, or go to the appeals page from the warning.
2. Read the reason.
3. Under **Your appeal**, explain what happened (at least 20 letters) and add facts that help.
4. Tap **Send appeal**. Another trust officer reads it and you are told the decision.

Applications and mobility cases you already have carry on while your appeal is checked.

## Common questions

**I didn't get the code on WhatsApp.** Check that you typed the right country code and number. Wait one minute and tap **Send a new code**. If it still doesn't come, tap **Use a different number** and check the number.

**It says the code is not right.** Type the newest code. Older codes stop working when you ask for a new one. After 5 wrong tries, ask for a new code.

**I can't apply. It says to add my trade.** Tap **Add my trade** and choose your trade in your Skill Passport.

**It says I already applied.** You can apply only once to each job. Find the job in **Applications**.

**My video was not approved.** Read the reason in **SkillShow**, record a new video that follows the rules and send it again.

**My photo or document won't upload.** Check the file type and size: photos JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB; documents PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB; videos up to 50 MB and 30 seconds.

**Someone asked me for money.** Don't pay. Use **Report this job** or **Report** on the message.

**Do I pay BLUESKILLS?** No. BLUESKILLS is free for workers.
