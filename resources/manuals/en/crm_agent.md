## Common questions

**A lead won't save: "This number is already a lead".** Open the existing lead instead of adding it again.

**I can't log a contact.** The worker turned off calls from BLUESKILLS, or the lead is marked do not contact. Don't contact them.

**The funnel numbers changed for an old period.** Steps after "Visitors" follow people as they are today, so they grow as workers make progress.

**I ended a campaign by mistake.** Ending can't be undone. Create a new campaign; its link will be different.
