## Common questions

**I can't change an invoice.** Issued invoices can't be changed. Void it with a reason and create a new one.

**The organization's credits didn't go up after I put it on a plan.** Plan credits are added when the plan's invoice is paid. Record the payment, or use **Adjust credits** with a reason.

**There are no plans to choose.** No active plan exists for that kind of organization. Create one under **Plans** or tick **Offered now** on an existing one.

**Are plan limits blocking organizations?** Not until pricing is approved and limits are switched on. Until then, credits and seats are only counted.
