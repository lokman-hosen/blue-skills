# Manual for institute owners

You are an **Institute Owner** on BLUESKILLS. Your training institute invites its graduates to BLUESKILLS, confirms their training and test results, groups them in talent pools for employers and follows their placements. You can do everything in your institute, including registering it and managing the team.

## Common questions

**I can't attest training or publish a pool.** BLUESKILLS must verify your accreditation first. Check the notice at the top of your workspace and upload what was asked for.

**A graduate isn't in the list.** Graduates appear only after they accept the invitation. Send the link again, or check the cohort is still open.

**The invite link says the cohort is closed.** Open the cohort and click **Open again**.

**Roster lines were refused.** Each line needs a name and a valid phone number, separated by a comma, and you can add up to 300 lines at a time.

**I can't see a graduate's documents or phone number.** Institutes never can. This protects the graduate.

**Who are the other roles?** A **Trainer** manages courses and cohorts and attests training. An **Assessor** records assessments and attests training. A **Placement Officer** invites graduates, manages talent pools and follows placements.
